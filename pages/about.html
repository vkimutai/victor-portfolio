My Vision $ Career Goals

I am committed to becoming a well-rounded full‑stack developer, with strong foundations in both frontend and backend technologies. My journey started with mastering HTML, and I am progressively expanding my skills to include CSS for styling, JavaScript for interactivity, and backend technologies for building dynamic and scalable applications.

My goal is to deeply understand how modern web systems work — from designing clean user interfaces to developing secure and efficient server-side applications. I aim to build applications that are not only functional but also user-friendly, accessible, and scalable.

In the long term, I aspire to work with leading technology companies such as Microsoft, Google, or Amazon, where I can contribute to impactful projects and collaborate with experienced engineers. I believe working in such environments will sharpen my skills and expose me to global engineering standards.

Beyond employment, I also plan to build Software as a Service (SaaS) products that solve real-world problems. I am especially interested in creating digital solutions that can improve businesses, education systems, and service delivery in Kenya and across Africa.

Ultimately, my vision is to use technology to solve meaningful problems — locally and globally — while continuously growing as a disciplined and innovative developer.
